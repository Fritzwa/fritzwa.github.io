Model Ratatouille: Recycling Diverse Models for Out-of-Distribution Generalization

Alexandre Rame et al.

Abstract in brief

The researchers start with checkpoints obtained by fine-tuning the same foundation model on different auxiliary tasks. They fine-tune each checkpoint on the target task and then average the resulting weights. This gives the models varied starting points while bringing them to the same task before merging. The authors report a new best result at the time on DomainBed for generalization to unseen domains.

A glimmer of weight‑space alignment

Yurij Mikhalevich

Editorial summary

The author reports that Claude inspected and ran Gemma 3 270M, identified attention heads that copied the user’s guess, and edited weights without training. Across three runs, one per setup, guess-following fell from 100% to 0–23%, at $27–28 per Opus 5.5 run and $48 for Fable 5.2. Unlike a model trained to ignore guesses, Fable’s edit reduced use of user information; Opus’s edits often substituted another wrong answer. All three runs anticipated reduced use of user information but neither tested nor reported it.

Merging Models with Fisher-Weighted Averaging

Michael S Matena, Colin A Raffel

Abstract in brief

Matena and Raffel study models that share an architecture and initialization. Their method uses Fisher information to weight each model’s contribution to the merged parameters. It improved on simple averaging in the authors’ robust fine-tuning and ensembling experiments. For intermediate-task training and domain-adaptive pretraining, it was competitive with gradient-based transfer at lower computational cost.

中文速读

递归自我改进（RSI） 当评测分数不再上涨时，光看总分很难判断模型下一步该练什么。作者提出的 AdaObs 保留原有评测，同时细分错误、提高题目难度，并加入相关任务，用这些反馈指导下一轮训练。在长文本阅读和指令遵循的 8 项基准上，作者报告平均分从 66.4 升至 73.7。在训练数据量相同的条件下，这一方法超过了只用原评测总分作反馈的基线。团队还用两种智能体后端做了独立测试，两者在 PostTrainBench 的 7 项任务上都取得了更高的平均分。

模型合并 本期未收录新进展。 经典选读：多个微调模型合并时，如果一个模型把权重调高、另一个把它调低，两边的改动就可能相互抵消。TIES 先把微调幅度很小的改动归零，再给每个参数选定一个方向，只合并方向一致的改动。作者报告，在他们测试的多种模型规模、任务和模态上，这样做超过了此前的合并方法。

权重空间编程 《为大语言模型的权重编程训练视觉模型》的作者让五个大语言模型直接修改卷积网络和视觉 Transformer 的权重，在四个数据集上完成改标签和纠错任务。作者用留出的图像评估了超过 2,600 次编辑，检查编辑是否成功、有无副作用，以及准确率下降了多少。对于常规训练后难以编辑的网络，作者发现，专门为编辑而训练能大幅提高编辑成功率。Edit-Ready Training（ERT）在训练时演练用闭式公式直接修改权重；对 GPT-6 Astra 和 Claude Opus 5.5 而言，ERT 的平均得分均高于常规训练、标签平滑和基于梯度的可编辑训练。

Sage-Evolve-Preview:Adaptive Observation for Post-Training Self-Improvement

Yinhao Tang et al.

Abstract in brief

The authors introduce AdaObs, which keeps a benchmark fixed during iterative post-training but changes the feedback used to guide training when progress stalls. Its controller refines diagnostics, increases task difficulty, and adds related tasks. Across eight independently optimized benchmarks in long-context reading comprehension and instruction following, the authors report a mean score increase from 66.4 to 73.7, outperforming anchor-only feedback under a matched data budget. They also report a higher seven-task average on the independent PostTrainBench evaluation with two agent backends.

TIES-Merging: Resolving Interference When Merging Models

Prateek Yadav et al.

Abstract in brief

The authors study two reasons that fine-tuned models can interfere during merging: parameter changes that carry little useful information and changes that point in opposite directions. TIES removes small changes, selects a sign for each parameter and merges updates with that sign. The authors report improvements over earlier methods across the model sizes, tasks and modalities they tested.

Training Vision Models for LLM-Driven Weight-Space Programming

Timo Ropinski, Sebastian Kranz

Abstract in brief

The authors tested five LLMs on relabeling and error correction in convolutional networks and vision transformers across four datasets. They scored over 2,600 weight edits on held-out images for success, side effects and accuracy loss. Training networks for editing substantially improved edit success where conventionally trained networks were hard to edit. Edit-Ready Training (ERT), which rehearses direct, closed-form weight edits, achieved the highest mean score for GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 compared with standard training, label smoothing and gradient-based editable training.
