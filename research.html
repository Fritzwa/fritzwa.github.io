递归自我改进（RSI） 当评测分数不再上涨时，光看总分很难判断模型下一步该练什么。作者提出的 AdaObs 保留原有评测，同时细分错误、提高题目难度，并加入相关任务，用这些反馈指导下一轮训练。在长文本阅读和指令遵循的 8 项基准上，作者报告平均分从 66.4 升至 73.7。在训练数据量相同的条件下，这一方法超过了只用原评测总分作反馈的基线。团队还用两种智能体后端做了独立测试，两者在 PostTrainBench 的 7 项任务上都取得了更高的平均分。

Recursive Self-Improvement

How learning systems change the process that produces their next improvement.

19 Nov 2026, 21:52 UTC+8Preprint · arXiv

Sage-Evolve-Preview:Adaptive Observation for Post-Training Self-Improvement

Yinhao Tang et al.

Abstract in brief

The authors introduce AdaObs, which keeps a benchmark fixed during iterative post-training but changes the feedback used to guide training when progress stalls. Its controller refines diagnostics, increases task difficulty, and adds related tasks. Across eight independently optimized benchmarks in long-context reading comprehension and instruction following, the authors report a mean score increase from 66.4 to 73.7, outperforming anchor-only feedback under a matched data budget. They also report a higher seven-task average on the independent PostTrainBench evaluation with two agent backends.
