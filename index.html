About Me

I’m Ziyi Wang, an undergraduate studying communication engineering at Hohai University.

My research interests include Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) and model merging, particularly for transferring capabilities across large multimodal models.

In 2027, I’ll continue my studies in information and communication engineering at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
